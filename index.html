Bagh Arena: a free 3D shooter from Bangladesh, in your browser

বাঘ অ্যারেনা বাংলাদেশের নিজস্ব ফ্রি থ্রিডি শুটার গেম। কোনো ডাউনলোড বা ইনস্টল ছাড়াই ফোনে বা কম্পিউটারে সরাসরি ব্রাউজারে খেলা যায়। পুরান ঢাকার গলি, গ্রাম বাংলার ধানখেত আর সুন্দরবনের কুয়াশাঢাকা ম্যানগ্রোভে বটের বিরুদ্ধে দ্রুতগতির লড়াইয়ে নেমে পড়ো।

Bagh Arena is a fast first-person shooter in the spirit of classic browser io shooters, built around Bangladeshi culture. Fight computer-controlled bots across Puran Dhaka, a Bengal village and the Sundarbans. There is nothing to download, it works on phones, tablets and PCs, and it is completely free with no ads and no loot boxes.

What you get

  • Three modes: free for all, team battle and a five-weapon ladder, each about five minutes long.
  • Five weapons with their own feel, plus sprint, slide and the Bagher Jhap (tiger pounce) dash.
  • Touch controls with a joystick and aim assist for phones, and keyboard and mouse on desktop.
  • Free cosmetics inspired by nakshi kantha, alpona, jamdani and rickshaw art, unlocked by playing.
  • Daily goals, medals and a progress backup, all saved in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bagh Arena free to play?

Yes. It is completely free, with no ads, no purchases and no random loot boxes. Every skin and reward is earned by playing.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. You can also add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

Can I play without internet or against real players?

You play against computer-controlled bots, so no online matchmaking is needed. After the first load the game can work offline. There is no online multiplayer.

Does Bagh Arena work on mobile?

Yes. On phones and tablets you get touch controls with a joystick, fire, aim, jump, slide and dash buttons. Landscape mode is recommended.

Is my progress saved?

Your level, coins, unlocks and settings are saved in your browser. You can copy a backup code in the Profile tab to keep it safe or move to another device.
