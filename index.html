হুঙ্কার Hunkar: Roar of Ekattor & July-24, a free Bangla 3D shooter

Hunkar (হুঙ্কার, "the roar") is a free first-person shooter that runs in your web browser at bagh.undo.it, on phone or computer. It is set in Bangladesh and carries the memory of two great struggles: the Liberation War of 1971 (Ekattor) and the July uprising of 2024. Fight in Ekattor Frontline beside the seven Bir Sreshtho heroes, in July Spirit beside everyday people of the uprising, or in Free for All, Team Battle and the Gun Rush (অস্ত্র দৌড়) mode where every two kills hand you a new weapon. The game was first released as Bagh Arena (বাঘ অ্যারেনা).

হুঙ্কার একটি বিনামূল্যের থ্রিডি শুটার গেম, যা সরাসরি ব্রাউজারে খেলা যায়। একাত্তরের রণাঙ্গন আর জুলাইয়ের চেতনা, পুরান ঢাকা থেকে সুন্দরবন পর্যন্ত দশটি বাংলাদেশি মানচিত্র, আর প্রতিটি মাল্টি-কিলে বাঘের গর্জন। কোনো বিজ্ঞাপন নেই, কোনো ডাউনলোড নেই, ফোন আর কম্পিউটার দুটোতেই চলে।

Rooted in Bengal

Fifteen guns are named after the rivers and places of Bangladesh: Padma, Meghna, Jamuna, Karnaphuli, Surma, Teesta, Karatoya and new ones such as Rupsha, Bhairab, Ichamati, Buriganga, Sangu and the Mahananda minigun. Carry up to four and pick them with keys 1 to 4. The game never switches guns by itself, not even while you reload. Skins are inspired by Nakshi Kantha, Alpona, rickshaw art, Gamchha, Jamdani, Tangail weaving and the Shapla, and each skin carries a small ability. Pickups are mishti sweets, chai and jhalmuri, you can smash clay pots (মাটির কলসি), and the menu music is a Bhupali raga with tabla. The home screen shows today's Bangla date and season, and your rank climbs from Sipahi to Shonar Bagh with insignia and service ribbons.

Ten maps

Puran Dhaka chowk, Gram Bangla village, the Sundarbans, a Boishakhi Mela, Sadarghat river port, Cox's Bazar beach, a Srimangal tea estate, Lalbagh Fort, a Muktijuddho camp and a Dhaka graffiti lane.

Play on phone or computer

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen joystick, drag to look, and tap fire. Push the joystick to its edge to run. Aim assist and auto-fire help on touch screens, and everything can be tuned in Settings. On a computer, use the keyboard and mouse: W A S D to move, Shift to run, C to slide, Q to pounce like a tiger, G for a grenade, 1 2 3 4 to pick a gun. Your name, level, rank, coins, unlocks, badges and settings are saved in your browser on your own device, so you can close the page and continue later. Make a backup code in Settings and update it now and then to keep your progress safe. You can also install it like an app and play offline.

Is Hunkar free?

Yes. It is free to play with no ads, no purchases and no random loot boxes. Coins and cosmetics are earned only by playing.

Who made it?

Hunkar was created by Ahnaf T. and is dedicated to Osman Goni and Abrar Fahad (ওসমান গনি ও আবরার ফাহাদ), and to the martyrs of 1971 and July 2024.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the game can be installed and opened without internet. Matches are against bots, so no connection is needed to play.

Who are the enemies?

Hunkar honours heroes and does not turn real people into targets. The opposing sides are invented archetypes, such as Hanadar raiders and Zalim guards, and the optional fighter Ojeyo (অজেয়) is an invented tiger-spirited champion for players who want a real challenge.
